How auto-sync works
Each dish is linked to its ingredients via Recipe Mapping (Module 17). When a stock signal from Module 18 drops below a recipe requirement, the dish is automatically marked unavailable and hidden from new customer orders. Replenishing stock restores it automatically. Staff cannot manually toggle availability.
